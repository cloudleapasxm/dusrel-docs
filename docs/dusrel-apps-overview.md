# Dusrel apps overview



Dusrel is an independent project building a suite of web apps and small browser tools. It is not a registered company. This page lists what is actually available today, and what is still on the way. Anything listed under "Coming soon" has no live version yet.

## Live apps

<img src="/docs-sections/dusrel-apps-overview-s1.jpg" alt="Live apps" width="1200" height="675">


These apps are live and open to use. Each entry links to the app itself and to its detail page on this site.

- **ShrinkIt** — Resize, compress and convert images in the browser. Live at [shrinkit.dusrel.com](https://shrinkit.dusrel.com). Detail page: [/apps/shrinkit/](https://dusrel.com/apps/shrinkit/).
- **Ricsline** — Make LRC lyric files with tap-to-sync timing and a built-in library. Live at [dusmamud.github.io/ricsline/](https://dusmamud.github.io/ricsline/). Detail page: [/apps/ricsline/](https://dusrel.com/apps/ricsline/).
- **ResumeForge** — Build a resume and download a print-ready PDF. Live at [dusmamud.github.io/resumeforge/](https://dusmamud.github.io/resumeforge/). Detail page: [/apps/resumeforge/](https://dusrel.com/apps/resumeforge/).
- **PDF Tools** — Client-side PDF tools, including merge, rotate, watermark and page numbering, processed in the browser. Live at [dusmamud.github.io/pdf-tools/](https://dusmamud.github.io/pdf-tools/). Detail page: [/apps/pdf-tools/](https://dusrel.com/apps/pdf-tools/).

## Pass tools

<img src="/docs-sections/dusrel-apps-overview-s2.jpg" alt="Pass tools" width="1200" height="675">


The tools at [dusrel.com/pass/](https://dusrel.com/pass/) are small single-purpose utilities. They run in the browser, need no account, and are free to use.

- **Password Generator** — Create strong, random passwords on your device. [/pass/password-generator/](https://dusrel.com/pass/password-generator/)
- **Username Generator** — Generate username ideas for social media, gaming and more. [/pass/username-generator/](https://dusrel.com/pass/username-generator/)
- **Word Counter** — Count words and characters in a piece of text. [/pass/word-counter/](https://dusrel.com/pass/word-counter/)
- **QR Code Generator** — Create QR codes with templates, colours and an optional logo, and download them as PNG or SVG. [/pass/qr-code-generator/](https://dusrel.com/pass/qr-code-generator/)
- **Age Calculator** — Calculate an exact age from a date of birth, down to the day. [/pass/age-calculator/](https://dusrel.com/pass/age-calculator/)
- **Password Strength Checker** — Check how strong a password is. [/pass/password-strength-checker/](https://dusrel.com/pass/password-strength-checker/)

This page is a reference, not a roadmap promise. A product moves from "Coming soon" to "Live apps" only when it has a public URL you can open. Dates and plans for unreleased products are deliberately not stated here, because they are not decided.
